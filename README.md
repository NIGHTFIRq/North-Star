# North Star
Low budget , energy, and space senor that uses vibration , sight , and temperature to predict and modater the health of industrial machines. 

Broad Question:
How robustly can a low-cost edge sensing system distinguish ordinary operating variation from abnormal mechanical conditions when speed, load, sensor placement, and sampling conditions change?

Main Technical Question:  
Under realistic shifts in speed, load, sensor placement, and sampling, what accuracy and false-alarm reliability can a low-cost edge monitoring system actually sustain—and which design choices preserve that reliability?
